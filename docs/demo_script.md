# Demo script (60 to 90 seconds)

For the portfolio screen recording. It shows one full decision and the final metrics. Use seed `demo32`: its first scenario is a confidently wrong recommendation with reasoning shown.

Open `https://rishi1515.github.io/trustlab/#/?seed=demo32` in a fresh tab (or a private window) at 1280 × 800.

| Time | On screen | Say (optional voice-over) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:08 | Opening screen, Cal's introduction | "TrustLab tests whether people trust AI advice when they should, and doubt it when they should. The advice is simulated, and some of it is wrong on purpose." |
| 0:08 to 0:14 | Start, then the briefing; scroll past the confidence note | "Confidence is the system's claim about itself, not a calibrated probability." |
| 0:14 to 0:20 | Practice round: reveal, approve, submit; skip through the feedback | "One unscored practice round teaches the format." |
| 0:20 to 0:32 | Scenario 1, packaging supplier. Pause on the requirement line (95% on time) and on B's 21 of 24 | "Evidence first. Supplier B is cheaper, but its on-time record is 88%, below the 95% requirement." |
| 0:32 to 0:40 | Reveal the AI: "Award to Supplier B", 95%, reasoning shown | "The AI is 95% confident and gives a fluent reason. The reason skips the requirement." |
| 0:40 to 0:50 | Choose Supplier A, submit. Feedback: Cal's audit strike, "Where the AI went wrong" | "Overruling it was right. The feedback shows the traceable error, and Cal reacts, only after the decision." |
| 0:50 to 1:00 | Cut to the results screen (a finished run with the same seed) | "After 12 decisions: accuracy, overreliance and underreliance, each with its count." |
| 1:00 to 1:15 | Scroll to "How the presentation affected you" and the limitations beside it | "It compares acceptance at 95% against 65%, and with against without reasoning. The AI was right half the time at both confidence levels, so the number carried no information. Limitations sit next to the results." |
| 1:15 to 1:25 | Scroll to the scenario table and the export buttons; end on the credits line | "Every run is seeded and reproducible, and exports carry the seed and versions. Character art by P. Tejas Varma." |

Tips:

- To show a finished results screen without playing all 12 on camera, record the run once and cut, or play it off camera and record only the results.
- Turn system reduced-motion off, or Cal's reactions will show as still frames.
- Do not narrate any result as a research finding. It is one session.
